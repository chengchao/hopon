# Music API terms for an entity: ElevenLabs via Cloudflare, MiniMax, Lyria 2

Research for [#127](https://github.com/chengchao/hopon/issues/127), part of map [#111](https://github.com/chengchao/hopon/issues/111); follows [#124](https://github.com/chengchao/hopon/issues/124) (`docs/research/elevenlabs-music.md` on `research/elevenlabs-music`). Researched 2026-10-16 from primary sources only (Cloudflare, ElevenLabs, MiniMax and Google pages, read that day); no API calls, no legal advice.

**Question:** #124 found ElevenLabs' Starter–Pro plans are "For Individual Use Only", so an entity needs Scale ($299/mo). Cloudflare AI Gateway sells `elevenlabs/music-v2` and `minimax/music-2.6` pay-per-use with Cloudflare credits. Which terms govern output bought that way, does the individual-use rule follow it, who owns the output and may it ship inside the app, what does 30 s cost on each route, and how does Lyria 2 on Vertex AI compare?

## 1. Cloudflare's route: what you are buying

- **Model catalog entry** [`elevenlabs/music-v2`](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai/models/elevenlabs/music-v2/): "Third-party"; Pricing "output audio seconds $0.0025 / Default (per second) $0.0025"; "Terms and License" links to https://elevenlabs.io/terms (the ElevenLabs Terms of Service, non-EEA); "More information" links to ElevenLabs' compose API reference. Parameters mirror `POST /v1/music`: `prompt` or `composition_plan`, `music_length_ms` 3,000–600,000, `output_format` incl. `opus_48000_64`, `seed`, `force_instrumental`, `store_for_inpainting`, `sign_with_c2pa`. No ZDR flag.
- **Model catalog entry** [`minimax/music-2.6`](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai/models/minimax/music-2.6/): "Third-party", "Zero data retention: Yes"; Pricing "Per track $0.15 / Per lyrics generation $0.01"; terms link https://www.minimaxi.com/terms. Parameters: `prompt` (≤ 2000 chars), `lyrics`, `is_instrumental` (default false), `lyrics_optimizer`, `format` mp3/wav, `sample_rate`, `bitrate`. Output is a URI to the audio file. No length parameter; it makes "full-length songs".
- **No provider account.** [Unified Billing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/features/unified-billing/): "Unified Billing works in two ways: through the AI binding or through the HTTP API. Both deduct credits from your account automatically without requiring provider API keys." "Call any model listed in the model catalog using `env.AI.run()`. This includes both Workers AI models and third-party models." The [binding reference](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/usage/worker-binding-methods/): "Third-party models require an AI Gateway and use Unified Billing." "You do not need to supply your own API keys." "Cloudflare manages the provider credentials and deducts credits from your account." The HTTP provider-native route for ElevenLabs ([providers/elevenlabs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/usage/providers/elevenlabs/)) is the opposite: it requires "An active ElevenLabs API token", so it is just a proxy for a direct subscription.
- **Price.** "A 5% fee is applied to all credits purchased through Unified Billing. For example, a $100 credit purchase will result in a $105 charge. Inference pricing from providers is passed through with no markup." No minimum purchase is stated; the balance may go negative and is charged monthly. So 30 s of music-v2 = 30 × $0.0025 = $0.075, effectively $0.07875 after the fee; one MiniMax track = $0.15, effectively $0.1575. At 10 games/day/account that is cents per day with no monthly floor.
- **Retention.** ZDR "only applies to Unified Billing requests that use Cloudflare-managed credentials"; the catalog marks MiniMax music ZDR and does not mark ElevenLabs music-v2, so ElevenLabs' default logging applies to prompts sent that way (#124 §7 still holds).

## 2. Which terms govern output bought through Cloudflare

### 2.1 Cloudflare's own terms

[Service-Specific Terms, Developer Platform](https://www.cloudflare.com/service-specific-terms-developer-platform/), section "Workers AI; AI Gateway":

- §1 ownership: "outputs received from the Services based on your Input ("Outputs") constitute Customer Content" and "you retain all applicable intellectual property or other proprietary rights in Inputs and Outputs to or from the Services." "Unless otherwise agreed, Cloudflare does not use any Customer Content to train generative AI tools."
- §2 responsibility: "You are solely responsible for all Outputs and Actions and acknowledge and agree that Outputs and Actions may not be accurate, error free, or operate as intended."
- §3 third-party models: "Machine learning models and search services made available by Cloudflare in connection with the Services constitute Third-Party Products (as defined in the Agreement)." "Your use thereof may be subject to additional terms between you and the model or search service provider (as applicable)." "you agree to comply with all restrictions, including any acceptable use policies, ... in the standard terms of the model or search service provider." "Cloudflare does not control and has no liability for Third-Party Products" and "has no obligation to indemnify you for any claims arising from your use of machine learning models". "any suspected violation of applicable third-party terms may result in immediate suspension of Cloudflare Services." It points to the Workers AI and AI Gateway developer docs "for additional information on applicable third-party terms", which is the per-model "Terms and License" link.
- §4 AI Gateway: "Certain AI Gateway features utilize Third-Party Products that operate on third-party infrastructure"; Cloudflare's DPA and security exhibit "do not apply to your use of such Third-Party Products accessed via AI Gateway. Please refer to each Third-Party Product providers' relevant policies for more information."

So on paper: our contract for the purchase, the output and its ownership is with Cloudflare; ElevenLabs' (or MiniMax's) _restrictions_ bind us by incorporation; neither Cloudflare nor the provider indemnifies us for the output.

### 2.2 ElevenLabs' side

- [Terms of Service](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use) (non-EEA, "Last Updated: 09 October 2026"): §4(c) "as between you and ElevenLabs, you retain all rights in and to your Output"; download permits use "outside of the Services" subject to the Terms and Prohibited Use Policy; §1(c) paid users "may use the Services for commercial purposes". Supplemental terms: "Any Supplemental Terms become part of your agreement with us if you use the applicable Services" and "the Supplemental Terms will control for that conflict"; the [Service-Specific Terms](https://elevenlabs.io/service-specific-terms) index lists the Music Terms and the ElevenAPI Terms. The second §17 "Purchase Through Reseller": "If you subscribe to Services from an authorized ElevenLabs reseller or partner ("Reseller") ... you will execute all orders directly with the Reseller and not ElevenLabs", "these Terms will act as the end user license agreement", and "Resellers are not authorized to modify the Agreement or make any promises or commitments on ElevenLabs's behalf".
- [ElevenAPI Terms](https://elevenlabs.io/elevenapi-terms) ("Last updated: October 09, 2026"): "These ElevenAPI Terms supplement your written agreement with ElevenLabs or, if none exists, the ElevenLabs Terms of Service." §1.2 the Music Terms are "Applicable Service Terms" that "form part of these ElevenAPI Terms solely as to the Services and models you access." §1.3 "Usage of the Services initiated through any Covered Functionality is billed under your plan or Order Form and counts toward the rate, concurrency, and entitlement limits applicable to you." Exhibit 3 (Music): "Unless you are an Authorized Reseller, you shall not resell, repackage, redistribute, sublicense, or otherwise make API access to the Music Service available to any third party." §2.3 a "Customer Application" is "any application, product, or service of yours through which you access the Services through the API".
- [Music Terms](https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms) ("Last updated: 09 October 2026"): §1.4 "Plan enrollment is restricted to eligible users as set out in the Music Commercial Rights Table." Exhibit A, Eligibility: Free, Starter, Creator, Pro "For Individual Use Only"; Scale "Individuals, or entities or organizations with fewer than 10 employees"; Business "fewer than 50 employees"; Enterprise "None". "Individual" is not defined. §2.2 "as between you and ElevenLabs, you retain any and all rights in and to your Input and Output." §4.2 "You must not, directly or indirectly, use Output to compile, create, or contribute to a Music Library." §4.3 Model Resale (including "offering any Music Model alongside third-party models") needs written authorization; Exhibit A marks it Custom for Enterprise tiers and Prohibited elsewhere. §4.8(i): a Reseller makes Music Models or API access available to third parties, but generating and distributing Output through your own product does not make you a Reseller.

### 2.3 Does "individual use" apply on the Cloudflare route?

The sources do not say. What they do establish:

- The individual-use rule is a **plan-enrollment eligibility** rule (§1.4 + Exhibit A), enforced at the ElevenLabs account. On the Cloudflare route hopon has no ElevenLabs account or plan: "Cloudflare manages the provider credentials". Cloudflare is the ElevenLabs customer, necessarily on a tier whose Model Resale is "Custom" (Enterprise), since offering a Music Model "alongside third-party models" is Model Resale needing written authorization. Which Commercial Rights row (media rights, download limits, Studio Game carve-out) ElevenLabs granted Cloudflare's end users is not published by either party.
- Cloudflare's §3 says we must comply with "restrictions, including any acceptable use policies, ... in the standard terms" of the provider. Eligibility for a plan is not a use restriction on Output; the use restrictions that plainly carry over are the Prohibited Use Policy, §2.1 prompt limits (no artist/song names), §4.2 no music library, and the §4.8 Studio Game carve-out, all of which hopon already satisfies (#124 §3).
- ElevenLabs' own Reseller clause (ToS §17) contemplates buying through "an authorized ElevenLabs reseller or partner" with the ToS as the EULA and orders placed with the reseller, which is the Cloudflare shape. It does not say the Music Terms' plan table applies to reseller end users.

**Who to ask:** Cloudflare (AI Gateway support, or the account team, citing the `elevenlabs/music-v2` catalog page) for "which ElevenLabs Music Commercial Rights tier applies to output generated through Unified Billing, and may an entity with under 10 employees ship that output in a published game?"; and, if Cloudflare defers, ElevenLabs sales/legal. The answer belongs in this file and in #52's disclosure. Until answered, treat the route as _probably fine but unconfirmed_, with Lyria 2 as the drop-in substitute.

### 2.4 Ownership and in-app redistribution

- Cloudflare: Outputs are Customer Content and ours.
- ElevenLabs: we retain rights in Output (ToS §4(c), Music Terms §2.2); the Reseller clause keeps the ToS as our EULA. One generated loop frozen into one game, served from R2 to players of that game, is distribution through our own product (§4.8(i)), not a Music Library (§4.2) and not Model Resale (§4.3), as long as Creators cannot browse or reuse tracks across games.
- Nobody indemnifies us: Cloudflare §3 disclaims it; ElevenLabs grants none on self-serve plans either.

## 3. MiniMax `music-2.6`

[MiniMax Open Platform Terms of Service](https://platform.minimax.io/protocol/terms-of-service) (client-rendered; "Effective Date: March 30, 2026"; provider "Nanonoble Pte Ltd.", Singapore):

- Written for businesses: registration asks for "name, contact person, email, phone number, contact address, and business registration documents"; it warns against "certifying a corporate Account under an individual's name". No individual-only tier exists.
- IP clause 3: "As between you and us, and to the extent permitted by applicable laws, you retain your ownership rights in Client input and generated content. We may use the input and generated content to provide, maintain, develop, and improve our Services, comply with applicable law, enforce our terms and policies, and keep our Services safe." No opt-out is offered in the terms (Cloudflare's ZDR flag on this model is the practical mitigation, but ZDR is about retention at the endpoint, not a licence term).
- IP clause 4: MiniMax will defend "third-party claims ... alleging that your or your end-users' use of the MINIMAX Open Platform Service under this Agreement infringes or misappropriates third-party patents or copyrights" (a direct-customer promise; whether it reaches Cloudflare's end users is, again, unstated).
- Prohibited: "(c) Independently sublicense, resell, or distribute any or all services outside of any integrated applications", so shipping output inside an app is the expected use.
- "AI Output" section: "You are solely responsible for all use of any AI Output by you and your end users".
- Governing law Singapore, SIAC arbitration.
- The consumer [MiniMax Audio Music Creation Terms](https://www.minimax.io/audio/doc/terms-of-service-music.html) (June 19, 2025) are for the app, not the API; they add that generated audio may carry identifiers you "may not alter or remove".
- Price via Cloudflare: $0.15 per track regardless of length, so a 30 s loop costs double ElevenLabs' and 2.5× Lyria's. The model is tuned for "full-length songs with vocals"; `is_instrumental: true` exists, but there is no length control, so a 30 s loop means trimming a longer track.

## 4. Lyria 2 on Vertex AI

- [Pricing](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/pricing), Lyria section: "Lyria 2 | Music generation | Generate music from a text prompt | Text prompt | Music | $0.06 / 1 count". Also listed: "Lyria 3 | 30 second music clip generation ... $0.04 / 1 count" and "Lyria 3 Pro | Full song ... $0.08 / 1 count", both with `-preview` model ids (`lyria-3-clip-preview`, `lyria-3-pro-preview`), i.e. pre-GA and outside any indemnity or SLA.
- [`lyria-002` model page](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/models/lyria/lyria-002): "Launch stage: GA", "Release date: 2025-10-27", "Maximum audio clip length: 32.8 seconds", "Maximum number of clips per prompt: 4", "Supported sample rates: 48kHz", MIME `audio/wav`, region `global` only; "Vocal generation", "Instrumental mode", "Audio watermarking", "Content Credentials (C2PA)" and "Filtering" are all "Not supported". The [generate-music guide](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/music/generate-music): "Each clip is 32.8 seconds long", request takes `prompt`, `negative_prompt`, `seed` or `sample_count`, returns base64 WAV; the guide describes generating instrumental tracks. Quota: 10 requests per minute per region per base model.
- [Service Specific Terms](https://cloud.google.com/terms/service-terms) §20 Generative AI: ""Generated Output" means the data or content generated by a Generative AI Service prompted by Customer Data. Generated Output is Customer Data. As between Customer and Google, Google does not assert any ownership rights in any new intellectual property created in the Generated Output." §18: "Google will not use Customer Data to train or fine-tune any AI/ML models without Customer's prior permission or instruction." §17: no using output to build competing models. §20(i): the generated-output indemnity covers only a "Generative AI Indemnified Service"; the [list](https://cloud.google.com/terms/generative-ai-indemnified-services) (last modified July 20, 2026) names Codey, Gemini, Imagen, PaLM and Veo. **Lyria is not listed**, so no output indemnity.
- Practical: a fixed 32.8 s WAV at $0.06 is a near-exact fit for a 30 s loop after the ffmpeg cross-fade (#124 §4), with no subscription, no individual-use question, no training and no artist-name prompt rules. The cost is a second provider in the Worker (Vertex needs a Google service account and OAuth, or Cloudflare's Vertex provider-native endpoint with BYOK). Lyria 2's quality against ElevenLabs is untested; the eval should compare them.

## 5. Recommendation

1. **Primary: `elevenlabs/music-v2` through Cloudflare AI Gateway Unified Billing.** No ElevenLabs account, ~$0.079 per 30 s, same request parameters as the direct API so #124's plan (34 s, `force_instrumental`, Opus, ffmpeg loop) is unchanged, one bill, and spend limits per gateway. Before launch, ask Cloudflare the §2.3 question and record the answer here.
2. **Fallback: Lyria 2 (`lyria-002`)** at $0.06 per 32.8 s clip under Google's terms (Generated Output is ours, no training), if Cloudflare's answer is negative or absent at launch. Add it to the eval now so the switch is a config change.
3. **Not MiniMax** for loops: $0.15 flat per track, no length control, licence to train on our prompts and audio.
4. **Not an ElevenLabs Scale subscription** at launch: $299/mo buys 1,993 minutes we will not use; revisit only if Cloudflare's answer forces a direct contract, in which case Scale is the first entity-eligible tier (#124).
5. Either way: keep one loop per game, frozen, no cross-game track browsing (§4.2), and name the actual provider in #52's disclosure.
